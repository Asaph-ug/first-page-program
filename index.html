Uganda is a top biodiversity destination, hosting nearly half the world’s endangered mountain gorillas, massive chimpanzee populations in Kibale, and the "Big Five" (lion, leopard, rhino, elephant, buffalo). Known as a premier birding destination, it boasts over 1,070 species, including the rare shoebill. Top spots include Queen Elizabeth, Murchison Falls, and Bwindi Impenetrable National Parks.

Top National Parks & Wildlife Reserves Bwindi Impenetrable National Park: Renowned for gorilla trekking. Queen Elizabeth National Park: Famed for tree-climbing lions, chimpanzees, and a high concentration of hippos. Murchison Falls National Park: Features Nile crocodiles, hippos, and savannah game. Kibale National Park: The premier location for chimpanzee tracking, home to 13 primate species. Kidepo Valley National Park: A remote, rugged park with a high diversity of wildlife including cheetahs. Lake Mburo National Park: Known for zebra, impala, and eland, easily accessible from Kampala
